MicStatusPro

Say goodbye to the "Am I muted?" panic!

MicStatusPro provides instant visual confirmation of your microphone status through customizable on-screen borders and overlays. It's a simple way to avoid accidental hot-mic moments during calls, meetings, recordings, and livestreams.

  • Real-time OS hardware mute detection
  • Global Hotkeys & Push-to-Talk (PTT)
  • Multi-monitor & app window tracking
  • Colorblind-friendly visual modes
  • 100% local—no audio recorded or sent
  • Built for Windows 10 & 11

Whether you're presenting to customers, joining a video meeting, recording content, streaming online, or conducting interviews, MicStatusPro helps eliminate uncertainty and reduce embarrassing hot-mic moments.
